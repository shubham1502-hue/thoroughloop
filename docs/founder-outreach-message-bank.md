# Founder Outreach Message Bank

These messages are for founder workflow feedback. They should not be used to imply validation, traction, customers, or sales activity.

## Cold LinkedIn DM

Hi [Name], I am testing a small public MVP called ThoroughLoop.

It is for founders whose operating context gets scattered across notes, calls, follow-ups, and decisions. The basic loop is: paste messy context, get one diagnosis, one action, and one decision to review later.

Would you be open to giving workflow feedback on whether that pain feels real? No pitch, just trying to learn if the loop maps to how founders actually work.

## Warm Intro DM

Hi [Name], [Mutual connection] suggested you might have useful perspective on a founder workflow tool I am testing.

The idea is simple: founders paste messy operating context and get back one diagnosis, one action, and one decision to review later.

Would you be open to a quick look and honest workflow feedback? I am not looking for compliments. I am trying to find where the idea breaks.

## Follow-Up After No Reply

Hi [Name], quick follow-up on this.

No pressure if now is not a good time. I am specifically trying to learn whether scattered founder context is a real enough pain, and whether a diagnosis, action, decision, and review loop feels useful or forced.

If you have 10 minutes, I would value the critique.

## Message After They Agree To Look

Thanks. I will keep this tight.

The feedback I am looking for:

- Does the pain feel familiar?
- Would you know what to paste into it?
- Does the output help you decide what to do next?
- Is the review step useful, or does it feel artificial?

I can send a short demo or walk you through it live.

## Message After Demo

Thanks for looking at ThoroughLoop.

The most useful feedback would be where the loop felt real versus where it felt generic:

- Did the diagnosis identify a useful bottleneck?
- Was one action enough, or too narrow?
- Did saving a decision to review later feel useful?
- Which workflow should I test next: sales, customer feedback, hiring, or investor updates?

Brutal honesty is more useful than encouragement here.

## Request For Brutally Honest Feedback

Can I ask for the blunt version?

If this would not fit your workflow, I would rather know now. The questions I am trying to answer are:

- Is scattered operating context a real founder pain for you?
- Does this loop make the pain clearer, or just repackage notes?
- What would make you stop using it after one try?
- What part would need to be much better before it was worth repeating weekly?

No need to soften it. The goal is to improve the workflow, not collect praise.
