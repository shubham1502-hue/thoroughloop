# Synthetic Demo Examples

These examples are fictional and public-safe. They are intended for demos, tests, and reviewer orientation.

## 1. GTM Pipeline Leakage

**Messy input**

```text
FinCore Labs has been stuck in negotiation for 21 days after raising a pricing concern. BrightLayer AI has not replied after proposal for 12 days. Northstar Ops completed demo but is waiting for internal review. Founder follow-up is slipping and proposal-stage deals need attention this week.
```

**Expected workflow:** Revenue Rescue

**Expected bottleneck:** Late-stage follow-up and proposal risk are more urgent than adding more top-of-funnel volume.

**Expected founder action:** Send one founder-led follow-up to the highest-risk late-stage account and confirm the next decision step.

**Expected decision to review next week:** Decide whether founder-led follow-up should focus on the highest-risk late-stage account before adding new top-of-funnel work.

## 2. Onboarding Handoff Breakdown

**Messy input**

```text
Northstar Ops is closed-won but onboarding is blocked. Customer success says setup is waiting on implementation details, the buyer has not reached activation, and go-live is slipping because no owner is clearly driving the next milestone.
```

**Expected workflow:** Onboarding Risk

**Expected bottleneck:** A closed customer is not activated because ownership and the next milestone are unclear.

**Expected founder action:** Contact the owner and confirm the blocker, next milestone, and date to activation.

**Expected decision to review next week:** Decide whether the customer needs founder intervention to unblock activation.

## 3. Hiring Process Confusion

**Messy input**

```text
Hiring for the first customer success role is stuck. Two candidate interviews are complete, the trial task feedback is scattered, the recruiter is waiting, and nobody knows whether to make an offer or restart the role search.
```

**Expected workflow:** Hiring Bottleneck

**Expected bottleneck:** The candidate decision is stuck because ownership and the next hiring step are unclear.

**Expected founder action:** Choose the priority hiring bottleneck and make the next candidate or role decision this week.

**Expected decision to review next week:** Decide which role or candidate needs founder action this week.

## 4. Product Feedback Overload

**Messy input**

```text
Three customers mentioned different roadmap changes. One wants better onboarding templates, one wants a usage summary, and one wants admin controls. The founder has scattered call notes and no clear operating focus for next week.
```

**Expected workflow:** Weekly Operating Review

**Expected bottleneck:** Product feedback is scattered and needs one operating focus before more priorities are added.

**Expected founder action:** Choose one operating focus for next week and close or defer the rest of the decision queue.

**Expected decision to review next week:** Decide the one operating focus that should receive founder attention next week.

## 5. Investor Update Chaos

**Messy input**

```text
The investor update is due before the board check-in. Runway, burn, growth metrics, key risks, and the fundraising ask are spread across notes. The founder needs one monthly update narrative that is honest without over-explaining every internal issue.
```

**Expected workflow:** Investor Update

**Expected bottleneck:** The investor narrative is scattered across metrics, risks, and asks.

**Expected founder action:** Draft the investor update around progress, risks, metrics, and one clear ask.

**Expected decision to review next week:** Decide the investor narrative and ask that should be shared this period.
