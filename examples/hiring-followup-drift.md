# Hiring Follow-Up Drift

## Messy Founder Context

The founding customer success role is drifting. Candidate A has good customer instincts but less startup pace. Candidate B is stronger on ops but weaker with difficult customers. The team feedback is in three docs and nobody has written the decision criteria. Recruiter wants to send more candidates. I am not sure if we should make an offer, run another round, or rewrite the role.

## Diagnosis

The operating bottleneck is hiring decision ownership, not candidate volume.

## Founder Memo

The hiring loop has enough signal to force a decision, but the role criteria and owner are still unclear. Adding more candidates will create more comparison work without resolving whether the company needs a customer-facing operator, an implementation owner, or a support lead.

## Founder Action

Write the decision criteria for the role and choose one owner to make the next candidate call by Friday.

## Decision To Review

Decide whether to advance Candidate A, advance Candidate B, reopen the role, or pause hiring until the role is rewritten.

## Review Prompt

Next week, did the hiring loop move to a clear candidate decision, or is the role definition still blocking progress?
