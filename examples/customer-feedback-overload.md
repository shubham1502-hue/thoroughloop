# Customer Feedback Overload

## Messy Founder Context

Three customer calls all pointed in different directions. One customer wants onboarding templates, one wants better weekly reporting, one wants admin controls, and support says the newest accounts are confused by setup. Sales wants the admin story because it helps demos. CS wants onboarding fixed first. I do not know if this is a roadmap problem, sales enablement problem, or activation problem.

## Diagnosis

The operating bottleneck is prioritization. Customer feedback is becoming a messy backlog instead of one founder decision.

## Founder Memo

The feedback has useful signal, but the team is treating every request as equally urgent. The founder needs to choose the operating pattern behind the requests before assigning product work. The likely decision is whether the next week should focus on activation, sales confidence, or retention risk.

## Founder Action

Choose one feedback theme to test this week and park the rest outside the active operating loop.

## Decision To Review

Decide whether onboarding clarity, reporting visibility, or admin controls should receive founder attention first.

## Review Prompt

Next week, did the chosen theme create clearer customer progress, or did new feedback change the operating priority?
